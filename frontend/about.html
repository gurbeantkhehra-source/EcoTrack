WHO WE ARE

About ecotrack

ecotrack is a frontend-based smart waste segregation platform. It provides a simple interface where users can explore waste categories, use the detection demo and view waste-related dashboard information. Our aim is to encourage responsible waste disposal and make waste management easier to understand.
